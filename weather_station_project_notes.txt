Weather Station Project:

Parts List:

Arduino Mega 2560
LCD Display
Temperature sensor

22 Gauge Jumpers


Specs:

Monitor Temperature, humidity and atmospheric pressure

Display Weather Data on LCD

Robust design safe to move without disturbing connections

Battery Powered



10/08/2025

ESP32 can read temperature and pressure from the GYBMP280 breakout board

Had to install a few libraries to get it to work. I also wrote a short i2c scanner script to determine the address of the GYBMP280 (0x76)

Adafruit_BMP280.h and its dependencies Adafruit_sensor.h, and Adafruit_BusIO.h

I used the method described in this youtube video to interface with the GY BMP280 https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=InNHrtnZgUQ
